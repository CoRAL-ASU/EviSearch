    Try it in three minutes

    1. Open the demo table: 133 columns for metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer trials, drafted from the column headers and one example row, then reviewed and locked.

    3. Turn a correction into a rule and see which columns it would change; then open Learning to see what your reviews changed.
